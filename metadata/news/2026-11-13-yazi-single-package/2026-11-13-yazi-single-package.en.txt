Upstream stopped publishing the yazi-fm and yazi-cli crates after 26.5.6.
Both binaries, yazi and ya, are now built from a single package,
sys-fs/yazi, which replaces sys-fs/yazi-fm and sys-fs/yazi-cli.

To switch to it, run...

# cave resolve -x sys-fs/yazi --permit-uninstall sys-fs/yazi-fm --permit-uninstall sys-fs/yazi-cli
